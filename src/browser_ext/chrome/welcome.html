Nee Download Manager

Your browser is now connected to NDM. Downloads you start are handed to NDM automatically, with your sign-in cookies, so they download faster and can be resumed.

Checking connection

1Click any download

Archives, programs, disk images, video, audio and documents go straight to NDM.

2Right-click for more

"Download with NDM" on links, images and media, or "Download all links with NDM" to pick files from a page.

3Grab videos

Hover any video and press Download, then pick a quality.

4Stay in control

Hold Alt while clicking to keep a download in the browser. Turn capture off, or off for one site, from the toolbar button.
